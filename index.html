A FEW LITTLE ANSWERS

Good to know.

Before you pick your pal.

How much is a Suffy?+

Minecraft Suffys—Zobo, Bob Happy Gast, Foxey, and Dolfoney—are $10 each. Original Suffys—Flowery, Caty, Pine Appley, and Flootey—are $11 each. Prices are in US dollars.

How do I order?+

Add your favorites to your bag, then download or copy your order list. Online checkout and delivery options haven’t been set up yet. Saving a list doesn’t place an order or take payment.

Will my bag stay here?+

Your bag and favorites are saved in this browser when local storage is available. You can also download your order list to keep it with you.

Are these photos of the Suffys?+

These are playful character illustrations made for the store. Actual product photos and product details can be added as the collection comes to life.
